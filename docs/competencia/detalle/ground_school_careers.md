# Aviatory – Competitive Research: Ground School / Question Banks (A) and Pilot Career Platforms (B)

Research date: 26 Sept 2026. Sources: WebSearch, WebFetch, and direct curl of homepages for `<title>` / meta description / hreflang. Prices are only what was seen on the pages (or clearly labeled reseller/third-party pages); "not public" where not shown. Third-party traffic data (Similarweb/Semrush) was not available; only self-reported figures are noted.

Legend: **[ES]** Spanish-language, **[PT]** Portuguese-language, **[LATAM]** Latin America-based.

- **Target:** EASA/UK CAA and FAA student pilots (PPL, CPL, IR, ATPL), plus schools.

ATPL page title "Practice Real Exam ATPL Questions | Aviationexam". Keywords: ATPL questions, ECQB, EASA question bank. Small sitemap (about 38 URLs) and no real blog. Traffic not available.
- **vs Aviatory:** Strong on the technical question bank, apps and brand trust. No English (ICAO) prep, psychometrics or airline-selection/interview prep, and no Spanish UI.

## SEGMENT B — Pilot career platforms (job boards, prep and community)

### B1. PilotsGlobal
- **URL:** https://pilotsglobal.com/ (registration: /account/pilot-registration). HQ not verified. English.
- **Target:** Pilots worldwide (airline, business aviation, cadets); recruiters.
- **Features:** Pilot job board (claims 1,200+ vacancies in 70+ countries), pilot profiles/CVs, airline reviews and ratings, daily fleet and hiring rankings, filters (hours, licence, type rating, authority, visa sponsorship), immigration guides (NIW for the US), flight school directory.
- **Pricing:** Free for pilots, per the AeroScout comparison. Recruiter pricing sits behind a sales form (not public).
- **SEO:** Title "PilotsGlobal - Pilot Jobs Worldwide". Meta: "The largest pilot job network." WebFetch returned 403, but curl worked. Keywords: pilot jobs, airline pilot jobs, pilot salary. Traffic not available.
- **vs Aviatory:** A strong job-and-data network with no structured prep (English, technical or psychometric). A partnership or integration candidate.

### B2. Pilot Career Center (PCC)
- **URL:** https://pilotcareercenter.com/ (jobs: /Latest-Jobs-For-Pilots). HQ not verified. English. Country pages such as /USA.

## Key takeaways for Aviatory
1. **Technical question banks are commoditized.** EASA question banks now range from free to €99–249 a year (ATPLQ €249/yr, Airhead £18–33/mo, ATPL Training €9.90/mo with an AI tutor, LearnATPL €99/yr), and FAA ATP prep costs $90–299. Aviatory should treat technical refreshers as a component, not the headline.
2. **Nobody bundles the three pillars** (aviation English, technical refresh and psychotechnical tests) for airline selection in Spanish. The nearest are Loop Aviation (Brazil: ANAC exams, ICAO English and LATAM/Azul/GOL selection), Lift Aviation Plus (Brazil, R$89/mo) and FlightDeckFriend (UK: PDFs and coaching).
3. **AI is arriving:** Sporty's ChatDPE (AI oral exam), AeroScout Avienne (AI interview simulator), ATPL Training's AI tutor, Piloto Brasil's Comodore AI and Espaço Aéreo's chatbot. An AI interview or oral simulator in Spanish would be differentiated.
4. **Willingness to pay in LATAM:** Espaço Aéreo's selection prep costs R$872 promo (R$1,284 list), Piloto Brasil R$199.90 and Lift R$89/mo. The UK assessment prep market sells £40–60 digital packs and £690 simulator prep.
5. **SEO playbook to copy:**
   - Airhead's competitor-comparison blog.
   - AeroScout's programmatic job and airline pages (about 2,300 URLs).
   - Pilot Institute's roughly 800-post blog.
   - AeroTest Chile's long-tail articles ("proceso de selección LATAM/SKY/JetSMART", "cuánto cuesta ser piloto").
   - Spanish keywords with weak competition: "proceso de selección pilotos Avianca / LATAM / Viva / JetSMART", "preguntas entrevista piloto aerolínea", "psicotécnicos piloto", "curso ATPL online Aerocivil", "bolsa de empleo pilotos Colombia".
