# Aviatory competitive research: Aviation English / ICAO LPR (Level 4-6) and airline-interview English

Research date: 26 Sept 2026. Sources: WebSearch, WebFetch, and direct curl of homepages and sitemaps (title/meta/hreflang pulled from raw HTML where possible). Prices are only what was seen on the pages; "not public" means no price was shown. Blog size = number of URLs in the site's blog/post sitemap (approximate). **Traffic data: not available** (no Similarweb/Semrush access in this environment). None of these figures are made up; where a fetch failed, it says so.

Legend: LTB = Language Testing Body (EASA-approved). SDEA = Santos Dumont English Assessment (Brazil, ANAC). TEA = Test of English for Aviation (Mayflower). RELTA = RMIT. ELPAC = Eurocontrol.

## Key takeaways for Aviatory

1. **Nobody combines AI speaking practice + Spanish UX + LATAM exams + aptitude/interview prep.** CaptainPilot has the AI and multi-exam coverage (English only). Loop Aviation has the three-pillar portal (Portuguese only, Brazil). The Spanish-speaking market is served by thin, opaque players: Waypoint, Alas Educa, Aviation English Colombia, and English For Aviation Argentina.
2. **Price anchors (Sept 2026):** Online EASA test €159-€239; simulation €59-€69; tutoring €29-€80/h (Preply $13-$32/lesson); self-paced Spanish course €115 (Cinetic Plus); AI app $8.99/mo or $59.99/yr (Going Around); AI LMS $50/mo up to $300 for 3-6 months (CaptainPilot); Brazil hybrid R$1,997 (IcaoGo); Brazil portal R$49.90/mo (Loop).
3. **SEO:** Level6Aviation dominates "ICAO English test online" with about 826 blog URLs and 19 locales (including es-ES, but no LATAM Spanish variant such as es-CO or es-MX). Spanish SERPs ("inglés aeronáutico", "inglés OACI nivel 4") are fragmented among schools and Emagister directories, with no dominant content hub. The best on-page pattern to copy is one page per test part (ICAO Exam Practice) plus one page per exam type (CaptainPilot).
4. **Conversion hooks worth borrowing:** pay-for-your-exam if you score 3 (aviationenglish.com.br), free mock or trial (Ico, ICAO Exam Practice), certificate in 12-24 h (LTBs), and bundling course + exam + app (ICAO4U €449).
5. **Airline-interview English** is sold explicitly only by Ico Aviation English (R$1,550), English4ICAO, CaptainPilot (coaching tier) and Aviation English Asia. It's an open niche in Spanish.
