# Competitive research: pilot aptitude, psychometric and psychotechnical test prep

Research date: 26 Sept 2026. Sources: live fetches of homepages and pricing pages (curl plus WebFetch) and web search snippets. Prices are only the ones actually seen on a page. "Not public" means no price was visible. Traffic data wasn't available (Similarweb's API blocked the request), so SEO notes rely on the page `<title>`, the meta description, hreflang tags and sitemap counts.

Segment codes:
- **A**: dedicated online aptitude-test simulator
- **B**: generic psychometric prep with pilot packs
- **C**: test publisher's own official practice
- **D**: app-store app
- **E**: human coaching or selection-prep course (often mostly technical)
- **F**: content or free tools

## 1. PilotAptitudeTest.com (Segment A), the global market leader
- **URL:** https://pilotaptitudetest.com/ · Pricing: https://pilotaptitudetest.com/purchase
- **Country:** UK (founded 2011). **Language:** English only (en-GB), no hreflang.
- **Target:** cadets, direct-entry FOs and captains, worldwide. Covers 100+ assessments and 150+ airlines, schools and cadet schemes (BA, Emirates, Ryanair, Lufthansa, KLM, Cathay, Qatar, SIA...).
- **Features:**
  - 190+ activities, browser-based, works on PC, Mac, iOS and Android.
  - Airline-specific guides and profiles.
  - Live benchmarking and predictive scoring.
  - "CoPilot Wizard" for personalized plans and adaptive exams.
  - USB joystick plug-and-play.
  - Group-exercise simulator, personality-test simulator (8 formats) and flight-test simulator.
  - Pre-recorded video interview simulator with AI feedback.
  - "Crew Room" forum (community) and one-on-one support.
  - Claims 30,000–35,000 users since 2011.
- **Pricing (GBP, one-off access passes):** £29 for 7 days, £49 for 1 month, £69 for 3 months. All plans include everything.
- **SEO:**
  - Title: "Pilot Assessment Prep Software » PilotAptitudeTest.com ✈️👨‍✈️🕹️"
  - Meta: "Prepare for 100+ pilot assessments using the original preparation software for Airlines, Flying Schools and Cadet Schemes - 30,000+ users since 2011."
  - Heavy programmatic SEO: about 709 pages in the page sitemap (airline and assessment guides such as "PILAPT Assessment Guide 2026", "COMPASS Assessment Guide 2026") and 40 blog posts.
  - Keywords: pilot aptitude test, [airline] pilot assessment, COMPASS, PILAPT, Cut-e.
- **Vs Aviatory:**
  - Strengths: the deepest airline-specific coverage, very strong SEO on "[airline] pilot assessment", and a low entry price.
  - Weaknesses: English only, and no real aviation English or technical courses. That leaves a gap for a bundled platform in Spanish or Portuguese.

## Spanish, Portuguese and LATAM players

**Key finding:** there's no dedicated Spanish-language online pilot-aptitude simulator (no Pilotest or AvioTest equivalent).

## Takeaways for Aviatory
1. **Price anchors (pure aptitude):**
   - Free (Pilotest, SkyStudy beta).
   - About €30–50 per month (PilotAptitudeTest £49/month; Pilotentest €49/month; JobTestPrep £59/month).
   - About €70–150 for 3 months.
   - Official ADAPT bundles cost up to about £250 with personality.
   - LATAM benchmarks: R$89/month (Lift), 299₺/month (AvioTest, the local-currency app model).
2. **Spanish and Portuguese gap:** no Spanish- or Portuguese-language interactive simulator with airline-specific profiles exists, whether for Avianca, LATAM, Aeroméxico, Copa, Volaris, JetSMART, Iberia, Vueling, Air Europa, GOL or Azul. Spanish SERPs are served by JobTestPrep's English material, PDF sites and flight-school blogs.
3. **Bundles are rare and poorly executed:**
   - PASS is low-quality.
   - SkyStudy is English/EASA and still in beta.
   - Brazilian preparatórios lack psychotech.
   - Aviatory's aptitude + aviation English + courses bundle in ES/PT is open ground.
4. **SEO pattern that works:** programmatic "[Airline] pilot assessment / psicotécnicos [aerolínea]" pages (PilotAptitudeTest ~709 pages, AvioTest airline career pages, Psicotecnica.lat company pages).
5. **Partnership options:** Symbiotics (ADAPT) and Mollymawk sell or licence official practice material. WASIM-type sim and interview centers could be referral partners.
