# Competitor research: Airline pilot interview & selection prep

Segment: interview coaching, HR/technical question banks, sim assessment prep, group exercises, airline "gouge", CV/application help, full selection bundles.
Research date: 26 Sept 2026. All prices are as seen on the sites that day (or in search snippets where noted). "Not public" means no price was visible.
Method: homepage `<title>`/meta pulled with curl; sitemaps counted for content size; pricing pages fetched. Similarweb returned 403 for every request, so no traffic numbers were available.

**Shortlisted suggestions I could not verify as interview-prep businesses:** PilotBoost (domain for sale on Atom), Pilot Career Booster, Pilot Job Prep / pilotinterviewprep.com / pilotjobprep.com (empty pages), AeroCrew (only Aero Crew News, a magazine), Pilot Pathways (an airline pathway term, e.g. Republic; pilotpathways.com was intermittent and showed no product), ACE Aviation (aceaviation.co.uk is an Indian microlight maker), Cockpit Interview (domain unreachable), Interview Doctor (general career coaching, not aviation), PilotsGlobal and Pilotjobsnetwork (job boards with no interview-prep product found), Pilot Institute (ground school only, no interview product). Airline Apps (airlineapps.com) is the US application portal airlines use, not a prep product. Skytest is covered in the psychotechnical segment.

## Key takeaways for Aviatory
1. **Only ASP (France) and FlightDeckIQ (Gulf) bundle aviation English with interview prep.** ASP charges about €300 for English and €1,290–1,500 for interview. FlightDeckIQ scores ICAO English in AI video. No one does ICAO/FCL.055 English, full courses and psychotech well in one subscription.
2. **AI mock interviews are now table stakes** (VTH, Spitfire "Blaze", Rotate, YPI, FlightDeckIQ, PilotAptitudeTest). Aviatory needs one at launch.
3. **Price bands:** self-serve platforms run $25–$40/month or $55–$180 one-time per selection cycle. Airline packs cost €25–€30. Human coaching costs $200–$250/hr, US bundles $700–$1,300, and premium concierge $3,300–$5,900.
4. **Spanish, Portuguese and LATAM are largely unserved.** Every competitor is English-only except ASP (EN/FR). Only Rotate (LATAM/Avianca/Copa pack) and LPJ (Copa) cover LATAM carriers.
5. **SEO playbook that works:** programmatic "[Airline] pilot interview questions / assessment" pages (PilotAptitudeTest about 709 pages, Rotate about 2,500, Airmappr about 500, VTH 56 company pages) plus a steady blog (Spitfire about 261 posts). Several incumbents have weak on-page SEO (ASP generic title, Aviator Intelligence empty title, King Schools broken meta, AviationInterviews and LPJ with no meta description).
